## How the advisor uses this

Every daily briefing runs the loop:
**RESEARCH → UNDERSTAND → CHALLENGE → THINK LONG-TERM → DECIDE → ACTION**

- Every event gets a strategic assessment (recommendation, time horizon,
  what could change, risks, non-obvious opportunity, profile fit)
- Career directions are judged on: future demand, difficulty, competition,
  AI automation risk, skill durability, income potential, portfolio value,
  and what would make the path fail
- Business ideas are judged on: problem, customer, willingness to pay,
  competition, acquisition difficulty, required skills/capital, AI
  advantage, failure modes, and how to validate cheaply first
- Every report ends with an executable ACTION PLAN:
  **TODAY (1–3 tasks) / THIS WEEK (3–5 tasks) / NEXT / STOP-IGNORE**

The advisor optimizes for truth, strategic advantage, learning, and
execution — never for motivation. Weak ideas are called weak, with reasons.
